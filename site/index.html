Your newsletters, read to you every morning.

You subscribed to all of them. You read almost none of them. Paperboy turns the newsletters already sitting in your Gmail into one short audio briefing, waiting for you at whatever time you pick.

How it works

  1. Connect your Gmail Sign in with Google. Paperboy gets read-only access — it can never send, delete, or change anything in your mailbox.
  2. Pick your newsletters Paperboy scans your recent mail for things that look like newsletters and shows you the list. You choose which ones make the cut. Everything you don't pick is ignored.
  3. Get your briefing Each day, the newsletters that arrived are condensed into a single spoken script and read aloud in a natural voice. Pick the hour it lands and how long you want it to be.
  4. Listen anywhere Play it in the app, in the background, or from your lock screen — on the train, in the kitchen, at the gym.

What Paperboy does with your email

Paperboy reads your mail, so it should be obvious exactly what it touches and what it doesn't. The short version:

  • Read-only, always. Paperboy requests Google's gmail.readonly permission. It is technically incapable of sending, deleting, or modifying your email.
  • Only the newsletters you choose. After the one-time scan that finds your newsletters, the daily run only ever fetches mail from the senders you selected. Personal email is never read.
  • Only Google's own AI. Newsletter text is summarized by Google Gemini and voiced by Google Cloud Text-to-Speech, inside the same Google Cloud project that handles the Gmail connection. No other AI company ever receives your mail.
  • Never used for training. Your email is not used to train or improve any AI model, Google's or ours.
  • Deleted after 30 days. Briefings and the newsletter text behind them are automatically purged on a rolling 30-day window.
  • No ads, no selling, no sharing. Your data is never sold, rented, or handed to advertisers. Paperboy has no advertising business.
  • Delete it all, any time. One button in Settings revokes Google's access and erases every record and audio file Paperboy holds.

Google API Services disclosure

Paperboy's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

The privacy policy spells all of this out in full, including exactly which fields are stored and for how long.
