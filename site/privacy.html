Privacy Policy

Last updated 31 August 2026 · Effective 31 August 2026

Paperboy reads your email in order to work, so this policy is written to be read rather than skimmed past. It describes precisely what Paperboy accesses, what it stores, who else sees it, how long it is kept, and how to get rid of it.

Paperboy ("we", "us") is an iOS app operated by Sameer Bandha, an independent developer. Contact: support@paperboyhq.com.

1. The short version

  • Paperboy has read-only access to Gmail. It cannot send, delete, or modify your mail.
  • After the initial scan that finds your newsletters, it only fetches mail from the senders you selected.
  • Newsletter text is processed by Google Gemini and Google Cloud Text-to-Speech — no other AI provider.
  • Your email is never used to train AI models, ours or anyone else's.
  • Briefings and the newsletter text behind them are automatically deleted after 30 days.
  • We never sell, rent, or share your data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • No human at Paperboy reads your email.
  • You can erase everything from Settings in the app, at any time.

Paperboy has no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no third-party trackers. It does not collect your location, contacts, device identifiers, or browsing activity, and it does not track you across other apps or websites.

3. What we read from Gmail

Paperboy requests three Google permissions: gmail.readonly, userinfo.email, and userinfo.profile. The Gmail permission is read-only; the app has no technical ability to send, delete, archive, label, or alter anything in your mailbox.

Finding your newsletters (one-time, and when you refresh the list)

Paperboy looks at up to 50 messages from the last 30 days and identifies which are newsletters — a message counts if it carries the standard List-Unsubscribe header used by bulk senders, or if "newsletter" or "digest" appears in the sender or subject. From these, Paperboy keeps only the sender name and address so it can show you a list. The contents of messages examined during this scan are not stored.

Building your daily briefing (every day)

Once you've chosen your newsletters, the daily run queries Gmail only for messages from those specific senders, from the last 24 hours, up to 25 per sender. Nothing else in your mailbox is requested. Personal correspondence, receipts, and any newsletter you did not select are never fetched and never stored.

4. How we use it

Your data is used only to produce and deliver your briefing. Specifically:

  • The text of your selected newsletters is sent to the Google Gemini API, which condenses it into a summary and a spoken script. Up to roughly 12,000 characters per run is sent.
  • That script is sent to Google Cloud Text-to-Speech, which returns the audio.
  • The audio is stored in a private file bucket and streamed back to your device when you press play.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to producing your briefing. We do not use it to train, fine-tune, or improve any AI or machine-learning model, and our providers are contractually prohibited from doing so with data submitted through their paid APIs.

No human reads your email. No employee or contractor accesses message content, except in the narrow circumstances Google's policy permits: with your explicit consent for a specific message (for example, if you send us one to debug a problem you reported), where necessary for security or to investigate abuse, or where required by law.

5. Google API Limited Use

Paperboy's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Concretely, that commitment means Paperboy:

  • uses Google user data only to provide and improve the briefing feature you signed up for;
  • transfers it only to the providers listed below, only to deliver that feature, or where required by law;
  • never uses it for advertising of any kind;
  • never allows humans to read it, except in the limited cases described above;
  • never uses it to develop, train, or improve generalized AI or machine-learning models.

If Paperboy were ever acquired, your data could transfer to the acquirer, who would remain bound by this policy; we would notify you first.

7. How long we keep it

  • Briefings, newsletter text, and audio: 30 days. These are deleted automatically on a rolling window by a scheduled job. You do not have to do anything, and there is no way to opt out of the deletion.
  • Account details, preferences, newsletter selections, and Google tokens: kept while your account exists, because the app cannot function without them.
  • When you delete your account: we revoke Paperboy's access to your Google account, delete every audio file, and erase every database record associated with you. This is immediate and cannot be undone.

Residual copies may persist briefly in our database provider's encrypted backups before those backups age out on their normal rotation. They are not accessible to the app and are never restored into it.

8. How it's protected

  • Your Google access and refresh tokens are encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM using a key held only by the server.
  • Your audio lives in a private storage bucket with no public access. It is reachable only through a short-lived link, signed for your account and that one briefing, which expires within 24 hours.
  • All traffic between the app and our server travels over HTTPS.
  • Your login session is a signed token stored in the iOS keychain, the operating system's protected credential store.
  • Every request is checked against the signed-in account, so one user's briefings can never be fetched by another.

No system is perfectly secure, and we can't promise absolute security. If a breach ever affected your data, we would notify you and the relevant authorities as required by law.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Change what's read. Deselect a newsletter in the app and Paperboy stops fetching from that sender.
  • Disconnect Gmail. Signing out revokes our access to your Google account and deletes the stored tokens.
  • Delete everything. Settings → Delete Account erases all of it, permanently.
  • Revoke from Google's side. You can remove Paperboy's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, independently of the app.

Depending on where you live — including the EEA, the UK, and California — you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent. The in-app deletion covers most of these instantly. For anything else, email support@paperboyhq.com and we'll respond within 30 days. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

For users in the EEA and UK: we process your data to perform our agreement with you (delivering the briefing you asked for) and on the basis of the consent you give on Google's permission screen, which you can withdraw at any time using the links above. Paperboy does not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined under the California Consumer Privacy Act.

10. Children, location, and changes

Children. Paperboy is not directed at children and is not intended for anyone under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has given us data, email us and we'll delete it.

Where your data is processed. Paperboy's server and providers operate in the United States. If you use the app from elsewhere, your data is transferred to and processed in the US, which may have different data protection laws than your country.

Changes. If this policy changes materially, we'll update the date at the top and notify you in the app before the change takes effect. Continuing to use Paperboy afterwards means you accept the revised policy.

Questions. support@paperboyhq.com — a real person reads it.
